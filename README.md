## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns to help businesses understand why customers leave their services. The dashboard highlights churn rate, customer retention, tenure behavior, contract-based churn, and the impact of online security and backup services.

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly affects revenue and business growth. The objective of this project is to identify the key drivers of customer attrition and provide insights to improve customer retention strategies.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **7,043 customer records** with information such as:

* Customer type
* Contract type
* Tenure
* Monthly charges
* Online security
* Online backup
* Churn status

## Key Insights

* **Total Customers:** 7,043
* **Churn Rate:** 26.54%
* **Churned Customers:** 1,869
* Customers with **month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn.
* Customers with **0–6 months tenure** are most likely to leave.
* Customers without online security and backup services contribute significantly to churn.
* Long-term contract customers demonstrate stronger retention behavior.
